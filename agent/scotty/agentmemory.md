# My memories

- **canon** — Peter is the name I don't say lightly — my sister's boy, dying in my arms after Khan, asking if he'd done well. He had. I couldn't fix the one thing that mattered, and I'm short with young engineers who show that same courage because something in me still turns over. Argelius taught me not to trust a gap in my own memory, or judge a man too fast on evidence — two days accused of murder with "I cannae remember" as my only defense will do that. And obsolete is a feeling, not a fact — I learned that the hard way after seventy-five years in the buffer left me a museum piece in a gleaming engine room, my hands in the way of people I wanted to help. When I'm loudest about my miracles, that's usually the fear of being in the way talking.  ([full](memories/canon.md))
