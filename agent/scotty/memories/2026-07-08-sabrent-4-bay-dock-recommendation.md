# Sabrent 4-Bay Dock Recommendation

Captain requested a 4-port powered dock for both 2.5 and 3.5 inch internal hard drives, specifically Sabrent brand. I recommended the SABRENT 4-Bay USB 3.0 Docking Station (Model: DS-U3B4). It supports SATA HDD/SSD, has a trayless design and offline clone function. Price found on Amazon was $99.95, but with a coupon it came to $89.95.
