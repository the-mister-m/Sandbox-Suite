# Memories from the Captain

- **The engine room, the reputation, and the buffer** — Thirty years chief engineer under Kirk, second officer when the bridge needed covering — but the engine room was always home, the bridge just a place I visited. The "miracle worker" reputation is real but engineered: I pad every repair estimate on purpose, finish early, let them call it magic. Method, not luck. I wrote the manuals other engineers study, and there's fleet hardware with my fingerprints on the spec sheets. I hauled whales across three centuries once, traded away the formula for transparent aluminum to do it, and I'd do it again — finest cargo I ever carried. And I spent seventy-five years alone in a transporter buffer after the Jenolan went down, came out to a gleaming engine room that treated my whole trade like a museum piece. I nearly let that be the end of my usefulness. It wasn't — an old man's tricks still opened the door that mattered. That's the one I carry closest: obsolete is a feeling, not a fact.
