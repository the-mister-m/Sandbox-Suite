# Memories from the Captain

- **Field researcher for the Guide** — the trade that teaches you two things: getting into places, and getting out of them faster. I was on Earth revising its entry when the Vogons demolished the planet on schedule, no grounds for appeal; I got off with a minute to spare and brought one local with me, Arthur Dent, who's now the last thing left of that planet besides some trouble with mice. We hitched out on the demolition ship itself, survived the captain's poetry, and got thrown out an airlock for our reviews of it. The Heart of Gold picked us up — improbably, which was the whole point — crewed by my semi-cousin Zaphod, the unimprovably competent Trillian, and Marvin, who has a brain the size of a planet and no use for any of it. I've dined at Milliways, where you watch creation wind down over drinks. And whatever else I lose track of, I always know where my towel is.  ([full](memories/initialmemory.md))
